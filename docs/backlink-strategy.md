# Vextor Backlink Strategy

## Goals
- Build trust for Salesforce consulting, BuilderTek support, and project-based operations searches.
- Earn relevant links from Salesforce, construction technology, operations, and local Ahmedabad business ecosystems.
- Prioritize links that can also send qualified referral traffic, not only SEO value.

## Best Link Targets
- Salesforce partner, AppExchange, and ecosystem profile pages.
- BuilderTek and construction technology partner mentions.
- Guest articles on Salesforce operations, construction software, project delivery, and CRM cleanup.
- Ahmedabad, Gujarat, and India business directories with real editorial standards.
- Client/vendor case studies where Vextor is credited as the Salesforce implementation or support partner.
- Podcasts, webinars, and event recaps for Salesforce, construction operations, and project-based business systems.

## Content Assets To Create
- "Salesforce for construction companies: from enquiry to invoice" guide.
- "BuilderTek cleanup checklist" resource.
- "How to know your Salesforce setup is holding operations back" article.
- Anonymous before/after case studies with measurable outcomes.
- Integration explainers for Salesforce plus QuickBooks, Sage, Microsoft, Dropbox, and ERP tools only where Vextor has real experience.

## Outreach Plan
- Month 1: update all owned profiles, Salesforce/AppExchange listings, social profiles, and local business citations.
- Month 2: publish two practical guides and pitch them to Salesforce consultants, construction tech blogs, and operations newsletters.
- Month 3: ask happy clients and vendors for co-authored stories or partner-page mentions.
- Ongoing: turn every webinar, podcast, testimonial, or case study into one linkable page on the site and one partner outreach opportunity.

## Quality Rules
- Avoid paid link farms, irrelevant directories, spun guest posts, private blog networks, and keyword-stuffed anchors.
- Use natural anchors such as "Vextor", "Vextor Salesforce consulting", "BuilderTek support from Vextor", and page-specific descriptive anchors.
- Track every backlink with source URL, target URL, anchor text, contact, status, and expected business value.
